Welcome email

For letting someone into Slate, once their access request arrives.

  1. Fill in their name and email from the request, and generate a temporary password.
  2. In Supabase: Authentication → Users → Add user, with that email and password, and Auto Confirm User ticked.
  3. Copy email and paste it into a new message from Slate's Gmail (Ctrl+V), to their email, with the subject Tu cuenta de Slate está lista.

At least 8 characters. They'll replace it at their first login.
