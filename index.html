How Slate works

  1. 01

    Find it

    Search any movie or show. Poster, synopsis, genres, runtime, seasons and episodes — Slate fills it all in for you.

  2. 02

    Pin it

    Drop it on your watchlist, start it, finish it, or let it go. Every title moves to the right list on its own.

  3. 03

    Love it (or don't)

    Ten stars, a few honest words and the day you saw it. Your taste, on the record — forever.

Good questions

Frequently asked

What exactly is Slate?

A personal tracker for movies and TV shows. Keep what you've watched (with ratings, reviews and dates), what you're watching, what you want to watch next, and group it all into collections.

Where do the posters and details come from?

From TMDB, The Movie Database — a huge, community-built catalog of movies and shows. Search for a title and Slate brings in its poster, synopsis, genres, runtime, seasons and trailer.

Can other people see my lists?

No. Everything on your board is tied to your account, and only you can see or change it.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Slate is built to feel right on phones, tablets and computers, and whatever you change on one shows up on the others right away.

Do I track shows episode by episode?

Slate tracks shows by where you are with them — to watch, watching, finished or dropped — plus the day you started and the day you finished. No episode checklists to keep up with.

How do I get started?

Create an account with your email, confirm it from the message we send you, and start pinning titles. It takes about a minute.
